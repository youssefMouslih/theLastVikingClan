Phase 7 - Deadlines, notifications, realtime
- Server migration:
  - app/supabase/migrations/0042_league4v4_deadlines.sql adds
    league_deadline_reminders (unique per matchday/team/kind, internal-only RLS)
    and the SECURITY DEFINER league_run_deadlines() runner. It runs every 10
    minutes via a `vik-league-deadlines` pg_cron job.
  - Open: SCHEDULED matchdays past opens_at become OPEN and their fixtures
    become LINEUPS_PENDING; approved squads get LEAGUE_MATCHDAY_OPEN.
  - Reminder: inside the final 24h, squads without a submitted lineup get one
    LEAGUE_LINEUP_REMINDER per matchday; the unique marker makes duplicate runs
    a no-op and survives deadline extensions.
  - Close: expired matchdays reveal complete lineups via
    league_maybe_reveal_fixture; a fixture with exactly one lineup becomes
    FORFEITED 4-0/0-4 with 3-0/0-3 points; both missing becomes CANCELLED 0-0;
    present lineups are LOCKED; squads are notified and the transitions are
    audited (MATCHDAY_OPENED, MATCHDAY_CLOSED, FIXTURE_FORFEITED,
    FIXTURE_CANCELLED).
  - The runner never invents duel rows or scores, and duplicate runs report
    zero transitions. It locks season before matchday before fixture to match
    league_lock_* ordering.
- Client:
  - app/src/competition/league4v4/action.ts: leagueNextAction derives the next
